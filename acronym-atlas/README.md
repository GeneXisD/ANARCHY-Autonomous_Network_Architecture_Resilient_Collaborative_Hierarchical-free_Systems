# Acronym Atlas

The ANARCHY acronym method expands technical terms into meaningful words and
then follows those words into established technologies, projects, standards,
protocols and historical systems.

## ANARCHY

A — Autonomous  
N — Networked  
A — Architecture  
R — Resilient  
C — Collaborative  
H — Hierarchy-free  
Y — Systems

## Research method

For every important term:

1. Identify the acronym or name.
2. Identify alternate expansions.
3. Identify historical meaning.
4. Identify technical meaning.
5. Identify related projects.
6. Identify standards.
7. Identify licenses.
8. Identify provenance mechanisms.
9. Identify prior art.
10. Record the relationship to ANARCHY.

The purpose is discovery and attribution, not appropriation of third-party work.
