# NIST Machine-Readable Publication Identifier

NIST PubID pattern:

<publisher>.<series>.<report-num>[<part>][<edition>][-<update>][.<stage>][.<translation>]

Example:

NIST.HB.150-1e2021-upd3.ipd.spa

The identifier demonstrates how publication identity can encode structured
metadata such as publisher, series, report number, part, edition, update,
publication stage and translation.

Source:

https://www.nist.gov/publications
https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/
