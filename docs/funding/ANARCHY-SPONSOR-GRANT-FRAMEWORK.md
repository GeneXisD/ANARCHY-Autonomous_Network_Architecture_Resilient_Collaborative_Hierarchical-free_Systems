# ANARCHY — Sponsor and Grant Framework

## Executive proposition

ANARCHY converts a long-running body of systems research into an open, reproducible and provenance-aware engineering framework for resilient, collaborative and decentralized infrastructure.

The project combines historical technical evidence with modern build, software-supply-chain, provisioning, archival, and device-tooling research.

## Why the historical corpus matters

The historical material is not presented as decoration. It supplies:

- prior technical experiments;
- implementation evidence;
- design precedents;
- compatibility constraints;
- licensing and provenance lessons;
- reproducibility targets;
- a record of how the architecture evolved.

The corpus is therefore part of the research asset.

## Grant-ready work packages

### WP1 — Research archive and provenance

**Deliverables:** chronology, artifact catalog, hashes, source map, evidence taxonomy, archival references.

### WP2 — Open-source compliance infrastructure

**Deliverables:** license matrix, SPDX metadata, SBOM, notice records, trademark audit, release checklist.

### WP3 — Reproducible build system

**Deliverables:** cross-toolchain configurations, kernel/module build recipes, dependency cache records, build manifests, test reports.

### WP4 — Provisioning and local repository

**Deliverables:** ANARCHY manifest format, package metadata, local repository services, one-click/declarative provisioning prototypes.

### WP5 — Restore research

**Deliverables:** documented host-side restore architecture, open-source tooling integration, image/partition inspection tools, validation harnesses.

### WP6 — Decentralized infrastructure

**Deliverables:** content-addressed artifact metadata, peer/distributed repository prototype, provenance-aware distribution model, RitualMesh integration.

### WP7 — Demonstration and technology transfer

**Deliverables:** reproducible demonstrations, technical reports, public documentation, partner-ready demonstrations, training material.

## Sponsor outcomes

Sponsors can support measurable engineering outcomes rather than an undefined research effort:

- reproducible builds;
- auditable software supply chains;
- open-source compliance tooling;
- decentralized artifact distribution;
- archival preservation;
- cross-platform build infrastructure;
- device restore/recovery research within legitimate security boundaries;
- open technical documentation;
- workforce and knowledge-transfer outputs.

## Evidence discipline for funding claims

ANARCHY will distinguish:

- what the project has already demonstrated;
- what has been reproduced;
- what is currently under implementation;
- what is proposed;
- what requires external funding;
- what remains dependent on missing evidence or third-party permissions.

This prevents funding materials from turning research hypotheses into unsupported claims.

## Sponsor due-diligence package

A mature funding package should include:

1. executive summary;
2. technical architecture;
3. 25-year lineage document;
4. evidence matrix;
5. license/SBOM model;
6. security model;
7. reproducible-build plan;
8. roadmap and milestones;
9. budget/work-package mapping;
10. risk register;
11. governance model;
12. intellectual-property and trademark boundary statement;
13. demonstration plan;
14. public-benefit/open-release plan.

## Funding principle

Funding should accelerate reproducibility, infrastructure, documentation, testing, and public technical outputs. It should not require the project to misrepresent upstream ownership or erase the provenance of the technologies on which it builds.
